How I work

Every figure traced to its source

Not a claim in a README: the War Room build compares its published numbers against the raw source tables on every run — 19 checks, 19 passing — and fails if they disagree.

Gates, not notes

A short table fails the build instead of becoming a plausible-looking chart. Where a figure has no second source (20 of 45 indicators), the dashboard says so rather than implying verification.

Caveats next to the claim

Each deck ships the caveat that weakens it — sensor saturation, spliced series, two definitions of the same word. If the number cannot survive the caveat, it gets dropped.
